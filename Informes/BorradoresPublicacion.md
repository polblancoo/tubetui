Texto:
I built a TUI for YouTube in Rust. What's different from other terminal players: it logs into your real account (your home feed, subscriptions, playlists, watch history synced with YouTube), plays through mpv (or inside kitty), resumes where you left off, and can translate the voice of videos in other languages. UI in English/Spanish. Early but usable; feedback welcome.
